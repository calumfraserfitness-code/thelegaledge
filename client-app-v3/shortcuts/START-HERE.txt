LEGAL EDGE — PREPARED STEPS PILOT

This file is prepared, not yet tested on an iPhone. No Developer Program membership or exporter subscription is needed.

ON YOUR MAC
1. Unzip this folder. Check that your Mac is signed into iCloud.
2. Open sign-on-mac.command. It signs the prepared shortcut with Apple; it contains no private key or Health readings. If macOS blocks the command file, open Terminal in this folder and run: bash sign-on-mac.command
3. AirDrop the resulting Legal-Edge-Steps.shortcut to your iPhone.

ON YOUR IPHONE
Add the shortcut. Paste your own private setup key from Devices & apps in Legal Edge and enter one exact Health step-source name. Garmin is often Connect. Run once while unlocked and approve Health/network access.

A saved_days server response proves receipt. Check the previous complete day against the selected source in Health or its original device app. Errors list available sources; they do not mean connected. Do not share your completed shortcut or key.

After the first upload is verified, add a personal automation to run Legal Edge Steps on your chosen days, with an App Opened catch-up while unlocked. iOS may block Health access while locked. Do not roll out to clients until these checks pass.
