Clear boundaries

Who can see what?

Employee & assigned coach

  • The employee sees their own saved coaching records.
  • The assigned coach reviews answers and adjusts the plan.
  • Private coach notes stay coach-only.
  • Check-in audio and review videos are excluded from employer reports.

Authorised employer account

  • Access is limited to its assigned organisation and pilot.
  • Reporting uses eligible participants and recorded consent.
  • Group thresholds protect small samples.
  • No individual check-in text, health rows or private recordings.

Use the real employer login for a contracted pilot. Use this walkthrough when presenting the product before enrolment.
