## Implemented

- Nutrition opens one date, using the client's timezone and that date's published assignment. No seven-menu chooser on the default screen.
- Daily calorie/protein/carbohydrate/fat targets use dated prescriptions first, the assigned plan second and existing client targets last. Missing values remain unknown.
- Compact meal rows open recipes in an accessible drawer. Existing measured ingredients, regional units, batch quantities and preparation are retained; no meal images or preparation times were invented.
- Personal recipe library searches assigned active menus from published weeks. Swaps stay within meal category; similar calorie/protein options are labelled using a 20%/25% tolerance (minimum 50 kcal/5 g). These are comparisons, not a claim of nutritional equivalence or allergy safety.
- Favourites and dated food selections/logs persist in separate tables. Selecting a recipe does not mark it eaten. Log/undo updates one dated slot. Original recipes, assignments and programmes remain untouched.
- Quick logging accepts values supplied by the client. Unknown nutrients make the corresponding daily total unknown rather than counting as zero.
- Weekly schedule and existing measured shopping list are optional. Shopping covers the coach-assigned week, not the client's dated swaps; this distinction is stated in the list.
- Five primary destinations: Today, Training, Nutrition, Progress, Coach. Check-ins, diagnostics and devices are grouped within Progress. Weekly scheduling stays under Training. Account/firm routes remain available.
- Coach roster loads independently of the optional completeness report. An eight-second deadline and a separate unavailable message prevent its failure from blocking valid login.
