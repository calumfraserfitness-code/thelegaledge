# The Legal Edge Client App Project State

This repository contains application source, database migrations and synthetic tests only.

Operational notes containing client names, programme details, contact information, account reconciliation records or other personal data must not be committed to source control. Store those records only in the approved private coaching systems with access controls and documented retention.

## Current compliance status

- The legacy static portal has been retired because browser-side password protection is not authentication.
- The corporate reporting path is aggregate-only and excludes private coaching records.
- Quantitative employer outcome reporting requires at least eight eligible responses. Five-person pilots are delivery-only service tests.
- Corporate invitations and activation are blocked until the mandatory launch checks are verified.
- Participants can withdraw from a firm pilot and submit data-rights requests inside the app.
- Bloodwork, genetic information and supplied wearables are outside the founding corporate pilot unless separately reviewed, contracted and consented.
- The onboarding agreement and privacy notice remain counsel-review drafts until the controller contact, processor list, transfer safeguards, retention process, insurance and offer terms are confirmed.

## Source control rule

Do not add real client data, live credentials, exported database rows, health information, call transcripts, photographs, signed agreements or production logs to this repository.
